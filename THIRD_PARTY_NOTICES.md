## EDFBrowser signal-processing components

Source: https://www.teuniz.net/edfbrowser/edfbrowser_215_source.tar.gz

Version: 2.15. Download SHA-256, independently checked against the publisher's checksum:

`164abb75c647eed1434882e19ee5e255475335caa8f86e1d8865f5b75767cc11`

Included under `Sources/CDSP/upstream/filt`: Pan–Tompkins QRS detector, moving-average filter, spike suppression, EEG powerline subtraction, windowed-sinc and Parks–McClellan FIR designer.

Copyright © 2007–2026 Teunis van Beelen, with exact per-file years retained in the source. License: GPL version 3. Files were copied from the release archive with original notices preserved. The native Swift reader, writer, CSV handling, display, session format and interfaces were written separately for this project. Z-ratio and aEEG treatment follow the equations and processing order documented in the upstream implementation.

## Verification fixtures

The application's bundled demonstration is generated synthetic PSG data. External verification fixtures were downloaded to a temporary directory, and are not bundled or redistributed:

- EDFBrowser official synthetic EDF/BDF fixtures: https://www.teuniz.net/edf_bdf_testfiles/
- PhysioNet Sleep-EDF Expanded, `SC4001EC-Hypnogram.edf`: https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/ (Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0). Dataset creator: Bob Kemp. Original study: Kemp et al., IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering 47(9), 1185–1194 (2000).

The native interface uses Apple system frameworks and SF Symbols provided by macOS. The application icon is generated by `scripts/make-icon.swift` and is an independent design.
